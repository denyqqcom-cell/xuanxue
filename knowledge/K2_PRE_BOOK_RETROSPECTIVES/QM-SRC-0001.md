The most important lesson from this retrospective is that the project has repeatedly been harmed not by lack of rules, but by **premature certainty, post-hoc repair and excessive interpretive freedom**.

## 2. Recurrent error families

### R1. Completion inflation

Historical recurrence:

- extracted text was treated as equivalent to full reading;
- partial chapter coverage was previously reported as `100%`;
- high confidence labels were used before page-level or prospective verification was complete.

Current correction:

- `COMPLETE` for QM-SRC-0001 will require genuine 57/57 visual-page review;
- packet availability, OCR/text extraction or structural mapping cannot substitute for reading credit;
- evidence count is not coverage.

### R2. Source authority was repeatedly confused with truth

The old `📖 / 🧠` separation was an improvement because it separated book statements from project inference. But its original wording still implicitly gave book-derived statements a high confidence status.

That is too generous.

New separation:

- SOURCE = faithfully represented from the book;
- INFERENCE = project interpretation;
- EMPIRICAL_SUPPORT = independently tested support;
- CONTAMINATION = outside information that may affect the answer but prevents clean attribution.

A directly quoted or faithfully paraphrased rule may have high Source Fidelity and still have low Empirical Support.

### R3. One dramatic miss created over-generalized replacement rules

The 2026-06-15 A-share miss produced useful warnings, but several repairs were promoted too far:

- `开门 > 值符 > 生门 > 星神` became a fixed global priority;
- `逢空 = 方向待定` replaced a previous mechanical interpretation;
- 'must read at least three macro news items' became a universal precondition;
- large-loss cases encouraged hard `凶格计分` thresholds.

These repairs may be useful in some contexts, but a single event cannot establish a universal ordering or fixed semantic replacement.

QM-SRC-0016 later directly challenged the idea of one global information priority and instead supported method-family-specific priority.

**Pre-reading demotion:**

- universal fixed priority -> DEPRECATED AS GLOBAL RULE;
- `逢空=方向待定` -> CANDIDATE / context-dependent;
- `凶格 >=3 = 大凶` and multiplicative scoring -> PROVISIONAL HYPOTHESIS, not established law;
- mandatory news ingestion -> AUXILIARY CHANNEL, not part of clean method-only validation.

### R4. 'Energy-first' became too mechanical

`情境推演法 v0.1` made a useful correction by insisting that strength/weakness, 空墓刑迫 and context matter before interpreting symbols.

But it then introduced deterministic shortcuts such as:

- 旺相 = full effect;
- 休囚 = half effect;
- 四害 = automatic discount/amplification;
- fixed execution order declared '不可颠倒'.

These are modeling conveniences, not demonstrated coefficients.

QM-SRC-0016 explicitly warns against converting 旺衰、入墓、击刑、五不遇时、伏吟反吟 and similar labels into fixed good/bad outcomes without the concrete question and combination context.

**Pre-reading correction:** retain state information as features; remove fixed percentage-like semantics unless prospectively supported.

### R5. Narrative improvement also increased hindsight freedom

The 2026-08-11 theory update added:

- relationship-chain weaving;
- narrative coherence;
- multiple plausible interpretations.

These improve explanatory depth, but they also create a new failure mode: after knowing the outcome, the project can choose the most coherent narrative and mistake retrospective storytelling for predictive skill.

**Correction:** competing interpretation branches must be frozen before feedback, with discriminating observations and failure conditions. Narrative coherence is a quality-of-explanation metric, not a truth metric.

### R6. 'Three validations' became a false graduation line

The project correctly rejected single-case validation, but then risked making `>=3` into another magic number.

Three independent prospective cases are better than one; they are not proof of robustness.

**Correction:** `>=3` is only a minimum signal for possible promotion to PROVISIONAL. Further promotion requires baseline comparison, misses, independence, contamination audit and preferably negative controls.

### R7. External context solved one problem while creating another

The 2026-06-15 market failure showed that blind isolation from known real-world context can be practically poor.

But the repair 'always read news first' creates a different research problem: prior information can dominate the interpretation, and the final hit may then be wrongly attributed to 奇门.

**Correction:** where outside information is permitted, use an ablation structure:

1. method-only interpretation;
2. freeze;
3. context-augmented interpretation;
4. record delta;
5. keep attribution separate.

This preserves practical situational reasoning without corrupting method evaluation.

### R8. One miss was sometimes blamed on 'system limits' too quickly

The 2026-08-10 weather review suggested that province-level weather may exceed the method's spatial resolution.

That is a plausible hypothesis, not yet an established system ceiling. The miss could also come from model error, poor question scope, weak role mapping, ordinary weather base rates or interpretation error.

**Correction:** convert causal explanations for misses into rival hypotheses instead of declaring them as system facts after one case.

### R9. Written knowledge and executable method diverged

The 2026-08-11 comparison experiment discovered a 值符 calculation error even though the correct rule was already written in the knowledge base.

This shows that `knowing the rule` and `executing the rule reliably` are separate capabilities.

**Correction:** implementation/paipan checks remain a distinct gate from interpretive theory. A correct narrative built on an incorrect plate is invalid.

## 3. Current model assumptions at risk before reading Liang

The following assumptions must **not** be protected from contradiction by the new source:

1. Prediction Protocol Freeze is sufficient in its current form.
2. Method-family-specific priority is always superior to a global priority.
3. Static / dynamic / nine-palace / comprehensive is the best or final problem taxonomy.
4. Situational translation necessarily improves predictive accuracy.
5. Relationship-chain narrative necessarily improves predictive accuracy.
6. Strength/weakness should always be evaluated before symbol semantics.
7. Role maps can always be frozen cleanly before interpretation.
8. The current distinction between setup method, layout method and time family captures all important school differences.

Each is a current model component or hypothesis, not doctrine.

## 4. What would count as meaningful counter-evidence from QM-SRC-0001

A Liang source claim becomes a serious method challenge when it does more than merely disagree with another author.

Higher-value counter-evidence includes:

- a clearly specified method that yields a different pre-feedback decision path;
- internally consistent examples where the current framework would systematically choose the wrong information layer;
- a role-selection principle that reduces discretionary choice compared with the present Role Map Freeze;
- a setup/layout distinction missing from current context fields;
- an explicit failure condition or exclusion rule that makes the system more falsifiable;
- repeated examples showing that a current project rule must be narrowed to a smaller scenario class.

## 5. What would NOT be enough to rewrite the theory

The following alone are insufficient:

- '梁湘润 says so';
- one memorable case;
- a rule that only works after the result is known;
- a different terminology for the same structure;
- a claim that depends on switching setup method, role, key factor or interpretation after feedback;
- a case where external background information is mixed into the conclusion without attribution;
- a coherent story with no discriminating failure condition.

Such material remains SOURCE evidence or a TESTABLE hypothesis, not an automatic method update.

## 8. Method delta already accepted before opening the book

The retrospective itself forces these updates now, independent of what Liang will say:

- DEPRECATE universal `开门 > 值符 > 生门 > 星神` as a cross-scenario law.
- DEMOTE `逢空 = 方向待定` from replacement truth to context-dependent candidate.
- DEMOTE fixed 凶格 score thresholds and 'multiplicative' claims to unverified heuristics.
- REMOVE fixed '旺相 full / 休囚 half' semantics from any claim of established method.
- RECLASSIFY mandatory news/background ingestion as an auxiliary-information channel requiring separate attribution.
- RETAIN situational translation only with pre-feedback branch freezing and rival interpretations.
- RETAIN narrative coherence only as explanatory quality, never as validation evidence.
- CHANGE `>=3 cases = validated` to `>=3 prospective independent cases = minimum signal for provisional support`.
- TREAT claimed 'system ceilings' from isolated misses as hypotheses requiring rival-cause testing.

## 9. Theory direction after this retrospective

The project's emerging independent theory should move away from a larger dictionary of fixed meanings and toward a **constrained inference system**:

`context definition -> method/layout/time-family selection -> role map -> eligible features -> competing interpretation branches -> frozen prediction -> outcome audit -> model update`

The innovation target is not 'a better way to explain any result'.

It is a system that:

- makes fewer discretionary choices after feedback;
- exposes where a prediction can fail;
- separates book authority from empirical support;
- allows context-sensitive reasoning without hiding hindsight;
- can delete its own rules when evidence turns against them.

This direction remains provisional and may itself be revised by later books and tests.
