## 3. New source-reading correction: content is not endorsement

The strongest methodological finding so far is not a new Qimen rule.

The book preserves and explains a large body of traditional deterministic material in 卷二、卷三、卷五、卷六, but in 卷十 the author explicitly criticizes several inherited components.

Therefore this source itself forces a distinction:

`SOURCE_CONTAINS(X) != AUTHOR_ENDORSES(X)`

and more specifically:

`TRADITION_RECORDED != AUTHORIAL_OPERATIONAL_COMMITMENT`

A source may:

- preserve a traditional formula;
- explain how later texts interpret it;
- reproduce a lookup table;
- criticize that same material as mythologized, accidental, superstitious or methodologically weak.

If the project extracts only the earlier rule pages and ignores the author’s later evaluation, source fidelity is incomplete even if every quoted sentence is accurate.

This is a correction to an older reading habit: **citation fidelity alone is insufficient; statement role inside the source also matters.**

No new schema field is introduced yet. First test whether this distinction can be handled by existing Evidence / Distillate semantics without adding bureaucracy.

## 5. 卷三 p94 onward — 克应 corpus is not reality evidence

卷三 begins with `十干克应`, followed by `八门克应`, `九星克应` and `三奇到宫克应`.

The visual pages contain numerous concrete deterministic statements about:

- marriage / pregnancy;
- illness / death;
- crime / litigation;
- travel;
- wealth;
- interpersonal events;
- specific persons/animals/objects appearing as signs.

These are source records only.

They are **not** converted into:

- modern fact claims;
- medical/legal/criminal prediction rules;
- empirical support;
- clean prospective targets.

Most importantly, 卷十 later attacks precisely this type of “动应” material as accidental anecdotal accretion lacking a stable scientific/lawlike basis.

So within one book:

`DETERMINISTIC_RESPONSE_CORPUS`

and

`AUTHORIAL_CRITIQUE_OF_RESPONSE_CORPUS`

coexist.

This internal tension must be preserved rather than “resolved” by choosing whichever side fits the project.

## 9. 卷十 p414-p418 — the source itself rejects blind belief

The closing volume is unusually important for project epistemology.

The author’s own critique separates roughly three problematic inherited areas:

1. mythologized origin stories;
2. subjectively constructed / anecdotal “动应”;
3. talismanic/ritual material.

The text argues that:

- Huangdi-era divine-origin narratives are historically unreliable;
- many omen-like “动应” stories are accidental, fantasy-like and lack a true lawlike basis, and should be removed from the Qimen principle layer;
- talismanic rites should be criticized/removed from Qimen proper;
- even the remaining time-space/numerical inference component cannot simply be declared scientifically established;
- deeper research and real social practice are still needed to distinguish truth from falsehood.

This is `SOURCE_META_METHOD`, not empirical proof.

It does, however, directly falsify a naive reading strategy in which “because the book prints a rule, the book endorses the rule as reliable truth.”
